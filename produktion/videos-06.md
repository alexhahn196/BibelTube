### Eingangsgebet (186 Wörter)

> Lord, I've been waiting on something long enough that I've stopped saying it out loud.
>
> It isn't a crisis. It's the same thing, still unfinished, one more evening. I've asked
> before, and I don't have a better way to ask tonight.
>
> So I'm not going to try. I'm too tired to make the case, and you have already heard
> the case.
>
> There is a woman at the beginning of this book who prayed without making a sound —
> only her lips moving, so that the priest watching her thought something was wrong.
> Nothing was answered that night. She went back, and ate, and her face was different
> anyway.
>
> That is what I'm asking for. Not the answer yet. The part where I set the thing down
> and it stays down until morning.
>
> Hold what I'm carrying. It isn't going anywhere you can't reach.
>
> Let the reading be company rather than instruction. I'm not in a state to learn much
> tonight, but I can be kept.
>
> And for anyone else listening who has been waiting longer than they expected —
> give them a quiet night too.
>
> Amen.
