I Know You're Tired… First Samuel Runs All the Way to Morning

For the nights when you are waiting on something and there is nothing left to do
about it before morning. The book of Ruth, then the whole of First Samuel, then
Esther — read slowly, without interruption, from beginning to end.

Nothing to keep up with. Let it play, close your eyes, and rest.

Focused on:
• Being tired of waiting
• Hannah, Samuel, Saul and David, told as one long story
• A quiet start and a quiet ending
• Sleep that lasts through the night
• Scripture read slowly, without music breaks

Read from the World English Bible (British Edition).
