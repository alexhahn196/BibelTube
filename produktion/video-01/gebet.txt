Father, my mind won't stop tonight.

It isn't even one thing. It's everything at once — the conversation I keep replaying, the list that got longer instead of shorter, the sentence I said badly and can't unsay. My body is finished with this day. My head hasn't noticed yet.

I'm not going to promise you I can switch it off. I've tried that already, and the trying is part of the noise.

So I'm bringing it to you as it is. Not sorted. Not solved. Just handed over, one loose end at a time.

You know how this evening went. You were there for the part I'd rather forget. I don't have to explain it to you, and I don't have to have it fixed before I'm allowed to rest.

Let John's account of your Son be the voice in this room instead of mine. Let it run underneath my thoughts until my thoughts go quieter than it is.

Loosen my jaw. Slow my breathing. Take the watch from me — you don't sleep, and I do.

Keep this house through the night. Keep the people I love, wherever they are. And be gentle with everyone listening who is lying awake for reasons they can't say out loud.

I'm yours. Amen.
